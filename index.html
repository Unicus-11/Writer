What is Consciousism, So Far about?

It is a theoretical and philosophical framework proposing that consciousness is the primary, generative substrate of reality rather than a byproduct of physical matter.

Who wrote the book?

The book is written and self-published under the single name Clovis.

What are the three core axioms of Consciousism?

The three fundamental axioms are: Consciousness is primary, The Absolute is real, and Subjectivity is sacred.

How is the book structured?

It is split into two volumes across four books: Volume 1 covers the physics and quantum theories (Books 1 & 2), while Volume 2 covers practical phenomenology and philosophy (Books 3 & 4).

Do you need a math background to read it?

No. Readers interested in philosophy, meaning, and ethics can jump directly to Volume 2 (Book 3) without any mathematical background.

Does the theory violate known quantum physics or allow superluminal signaling?

No. The framework explicitly preserves quantum causality and the no-communication theorem.

Does the book claim to have solved the "Hard Problem of Consciousness"?

No. It refrains from claiming a solution to the hard problem, focusing instead on establishing the mathematical conditions under which a consciousness-first ontology remains coherent.
